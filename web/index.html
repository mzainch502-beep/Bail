WELCOME TO BAIL

Make ideas move.

Describe a scene and tap Generate video to render and save it. Generate image saves a PNG. Preview & edit scenes lets you change scene descriptions before export.

Install on your phone

Android: open in Chrome → menu → Add to Home screen. iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

What’s free?

Illustrated scenes, animated text, images and video export run on your device. City, mountains, forest, ocean, space, desert, coffee and product scenes are supported. Other subjects produce abstract artwork. No trained AI model is bundled.

Export format depends on your browser (WebM or MP4). Keep Bail in the foreground during export. Some mobile browsers don’t support recording.

Your space, your files.

Motion projects, images and music stay in this browser. No analytics, account, ads or tracking. GitHub serves the app and may log network requests according to its policies.

Generation in this version is entirely local. No prompt or image is sent to a server.

Saved projects use browser storage, which may be cleared. Export a JSON backup for important work. AI footage is downloaded separately and is not included in project backups.
